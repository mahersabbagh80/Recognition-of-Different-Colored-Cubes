# Learning Record 0001: Adopt a fundamentals-first mission

**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** Mission decision

## What changed

Maher rejected the project-first lesson structure because it did not establish enough machine-learning and computer-vision foundation. He approved a twelve-lesson sequence that teaches general principles first and then applies each principle to the colored-cube project.

## Evidence

Maher stated that he knows basic programming, Python, C++, and object-oriented programming, while machine learning, computer vision, and ROS 2 perception are new. He approved browser-based required exercises, optional Python practice, 15–20 minute lessons, interactive HTML, and printable PDFs.

## Consequence for teaching

Begin from the meaning of a vision problem and image data. Do not assume ML, CV, camera-geometry, or ROS 2 knowledge. Use the project as an application and evidence source after the general concept is established.

## What remains unmeasured

No specific ML or CV concept has yet been demonstrated by the learner. The first lesson should establish the initial learning baseline through its exercise and explain-back.
