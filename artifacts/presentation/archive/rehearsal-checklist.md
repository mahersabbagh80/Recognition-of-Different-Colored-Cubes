# Timed rehearsal checklist

Suggested speaking budget: 30 minutes, leaving time for questions. The allocations below sum to 30 minutes. This is a planning estimate, not a measured rehearsal time; adapt it to the tutor's actual time allowance.

Prepared for optional later use. No rehearsal is required for today's document-preparation task.

| Slides | Target time | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| 1–2 | 3 minutes | Introduce the goal and verified outcome |
| 3 | 3 minutes | Explain the architecture |
| 4 | 3 minutes | Show why the first detector was unusable |
| 5–6 | 6 minutes | Explain the dataset and fine-tuning |
| 7–8 | 5 minutes | Explain validation and results |
| 9 | 3 minutes | Explain ONNX, TensorRT, and Jetson deployment |
| 10 | 3 minutes | Present the saved live evidence |
| 11 | 2 minutes | Explain the geometry-filter limitation |
| 12 | 2 minutes | Conclude and name the next step |

During the rehearsal:

1. Present without reading full sentences from the slide.
2. Mark any term that is difficult to explain in one sentence.
3. Stop only for factual uncertainty, not for small wording mistakes.
4. Record the total time after slide 12.
5. Rehearse the two weakest slides once more.
